About the Game

Arrow Escape is an Android puzzle game engineered with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Players navigate through hundreds of meticulously calibrated levels, testing spatial awareness and deductive logic in an atmospheric cyberpunk visual aesthetic.

🎯 1,000 Levels

7 difficulty tiers from Tutorial to Master, mathematically verified 100% solvable without deadlocks.

⚡ Neon Cyberpunk

Fluid multi-segment arrow animations with luminous glow effects and dynamic audio-haptic feedback.

🏆 Daily Challenges

Fresh daily procedural puzzles, calendar streak milestones, and rewarding achievements.

The Engineering Standard

How Sankalpa AppWorks approaches software craftsmanship, algorithmic design, and user-centric architecture.

Mathematical Solvability

Every level is synthesized via proprietary reverse-walk graph algorithms. We mathematically guarantee 100% solvable puzzle layouts with zero deadlocks and deterministic difficulty calibration.

144Hz Native Fluidity

High-refresh-rate pacing tailored to match modern OLED displays (60Hz, 120Hz, 144Hz). Sub-pixel vector anti-aliasing and zero garbage-collector stutter during dynamic particle flights.

Zero-Knowledge Security

We reject invasive tracking and data harvesting. Our utility apps operate completely offline with hardware-backed Android Keystore and authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption sandboxes.

Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

App Name: Arrow Escape: Logic Puzzle (com.azad.arrowescape)

Summary: Arrow Escape does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, analytics, or device identifiers. It is a completely offline, 100% ad-free puzzle game.

1. Information We Collect

None. Arrow Escape does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII), location data, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or camera feeds.

2. Data Storage & Local Persistence

All gameplay data (such as highest level reached, star ratings, and game settings) is stored purely locally on your device using standard Android local storage. This data never leaves your device and is erased if you uninstall the app or clear app data.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

Arrow Escape is 100% Ad-Free. We do not integrate third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.) and do not integrate third-party telemetry or behavioral tracking SDKs.

4. Device Permissions Used

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback when tapping arrows and completing levels. No private data is accessed or collected through this permission.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Arrow Escape does not address anyone under the age of 13 for data collection because no data is collected from any user of any age. The game is suitable for all ages (PEGI 3 / Everyone).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact the developer via Google Play Console support.

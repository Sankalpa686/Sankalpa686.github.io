Privacy Policy

Organization: Sankalpa AppWorks • Lead Developer: Sankalpa Mahapatra (Azad) • Effective Date: September 5, 2026 (Updated October 2026)

Summary & Core Commitment: Applications published by Sankalpa AppWorks (including Arrow Escape: Logic Puzzle, Snake Traffic Clear, and Secure Vault) are built on an offline-first, zero-knowledge architectural philosophy. We do not harvest, track, sell, or transmit your private data.

1. Information Collection & Use

We collect no personally identifiable information (PII). Our games and utility applications do not require user account creation, phone numbers, or social media logins.

  • Arrow Escape: Logic Puzzle: Does not collect or transmit any user credentials, location data, contacts, or media.
  • Snake Traffic Clear: Completely offline gameplay logic without external telemetry.
  • Secure Vault: All documents, certificates, and encryption keys are stored strictly in a local hardware-encrypted sandbox (AES-256-GCM via Android Keystore). No files are uploaded to cloud servers.

2. Local Sandbox & Device Persistence

All game progression (e.g. current level reached, coins earned, stars unlocked, haptic/audio settings) is stored purely locally on your device using standard Android sandbox storage. This data never leaves your device and is wiped if you choose to uninstall the application or clear app data via system settings.

3. Advertisements & Google AdMob

Where ad-supported versions or rewarded ads are offered, we adhere to strict Google Play Developer Policies and the IAB Tech Lab app-ads.txt standard (Publisher ID: pub-6918504259163973). Ads are served safely without invasive tracking or sensitive data collection.

4. Device Permissions

Our applications request only the minimum required permissions necessary to provide their primary features:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Exclusively used to deliver tactile haptic feedback during arrow escapes, collisions, and level completion. No private information is read or recorded.
  • android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC: In Secure Vault, used purely on-device via AndroidX BiometricPrompt for authentication without storing raw biometric imagery.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Our applications are intended for general audiences of all ages (PEGI 3 / Everyone). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Security of Your Information

We take security seriously. For privacy-focused applications like Secure Vault, all sensitive content is protected using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption with cryptographic keys stored inside the device's hardware-backed Secure Element / TEE (Trusted Execution Environment).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new application releases or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published immediately on this URL with an updated effective date.
